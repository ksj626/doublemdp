## Shared infrastructure decisions

- Offline datasets are immutable, checksummed arrays. RQL uses the original trajectory-uniform/random-start sampler; BDPO original-task checks retain the source scan-and-reshuffle sampler. Shared new-task BDPO diagnostics use explicit transition-uniform sampling.
- A terminal-bandit adapter duplicates the single terminal row for RQL h=1, retains its one reward, and disables bootstrap. A separately named `transition_h1` adapter is required for the post-action-reward maze: it samples all saved transitions within uniformly selected episodes and provides their actual saved successor. This preserves terminal success rewards and timeout bootstrap. It changes the toy data interface, not the RQL loss. Early maze runs without this adapter are preserved with `SCIENTIFIC_LIMITATIONS.json` and excluded from credit-assignment claims.
- Maintained-runner checkpoints include all parameters, targets, optimizer state, RNGs, dataset/sampler identity, phase, counters, and shard position; the DPPO port additionally saves simulator state. Original-source DPPO BC supports exact epoch-boundary resume. Its separate original-source online launcher rejects resume because upstream omits rollout-local and asynchronous worker state. Offline checkpoints are saved even though upstream RQL's saving gate excludes its offline phase. The unnecessary 100M-capacity mutable replay allocation is removed.
- Evaluation uses fixed independent seeds and never supplies training transitions. This differs from upstream shared global NumPy evaluation RNG and is disclosed. RQL retains the source per-environment-step chunk redraw/queue convention. Selection is the final checkpoint, never the best seed/test checkpoint.
- The large-data adapter verifies 100 distinct shard contents, byte hashes, actual processed transition counts and validation partners. Its sorted cyclic replacement before every 1000th update is source-faithful. Missing 100M data never falls back to 1M.
- Toy architectures, inner-step counts, reward scales, and budgets are diagnostic choices recorded in each configuration. These are not matched-compute or fidelity comparisons. BDPO OGBench and DPPO OGBench are new ports. DPPO online results are excluded from strictly offline results; its BC initialization can be reported separately.
- The maze's bounded geometric potential supplies extra goal information. Saved dense rewards are `r + gamma*Phi(next)-Phi(current)`, with zero potential at genuine success terminals; logging timeouts bootstrap and keep their potential. The discount must match the dataset's shaping discount.
- Native action latents are not physical positions. Traces retain actual times and unbounded latent ranges; only final decoded actions execute. RQL heads are expectile objectives; BDPO heads include regularization/pessimism. DPPO has no inner critic. Unregularized continuation probes are separate and do not assert monotonic improvement. Conditional Monte Carlo errors are distinct from uncertainty across training seeds.

## Runtime evidence

Fresh `doublemdp-jax`, `doublemdp-torch`, and isolated legacy environments were created without changing host drivers or global installations. Actual GPU matrix multiplication, gradients, optimizer updates and synchronization were recorded independently for JAX and PyTorch. JAX preallocation is disabled. The inherited unavailable NVIDIA package index was bypassed only for project installs. Tested CUDA 12.4 wheels are pinned independently of the system toolkit and driver banner.

See `RESULTS.md` for measured completions, failed trials, exact artifact paths and outstanding runs. The existence of code, a passing smoke test or a launched job does not establish paper-score reproduction.
