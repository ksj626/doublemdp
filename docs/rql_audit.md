Official source is [aoberai/rql](https://github.com/aoberai/rql/tree/229c956efb4494c2b9bb0bbddbd67b761c93f1cc), pinned at `229c956efb4494c2b9bb0bbddbd67b761c93f1cc`. OGBench is v1.2.0, commit `572788d97c2a6225b8edb7c420e304c04d1b3459`, with Gymnasium 0.29.1 as requested by the RQL README. The source copy is isolated in `third_party/rql`; the extracted code preserves the upstream MIT copyright notice, with a copy in `third_party/licenses/rql-LICENSE.md`.

The public `README.md` example gives humanoid alpha 10 / expectile .9; `hyperparameters.sh` and paper Table 3 agree on alpha .3 / expectile .5. The manifest follows the latter. `q_agg='mean'` is retained as an unused source config field; the implemented reduction is explicitly mean minus rho times population standard deviation for target values, and mean for the actor.

## Exact loss semantics

A batch has observations `[h+1,B,O]`, per-step actions `[h+1,B,A]`, and rewards, terminals, masks `[h+1,B]`. The action latent dimension is `h*A`. Sampling first selects a trajectory uniformly among trajectories with at least two rows, then a start uniformly in `[initial, terminal)`. It takes `h+1` consecutive rows with indices clamped at the terminal. `terminals` marks dataset boundaries; OGBench `masks` marks task-success bootstrapping and must remain separate, including at logging timeouts.

The duration batch contains B/2 continuous uniform draws on `[0,1)` and B/2 discrete draws from `{0/F,…,F/F}`. The source's discrete endpoint **includes F**; the appendix's discrete list omits that endpoint. Reversal begins at the dataset action chunk and time 1 and takes F backward Euler steps of size duration/F. Reconstructed latent inputs are stopped before critic regression. The value head is time conditioned and has ten ensemble members in faithful configs.

The horizon-reduced target is the sum of rewards with weights `gamma**[0,…,h-1,inf]`, multiplied by one minus right-shifted terminal indicators, plus `gamma**h * masks[-2] * (mean(target_values) - rho*std(target_values))`. The bootstrap state is the final observation row with fresh Gaussian action noise at time zero. The final extra row's reward is exactly zero weighted. The validity weight is `sum(right_shifted_terminals) <= 1`; thus chunks with multiple repeated terminal rows are suppressed in source critic/actor losses. This is preserved rather than replaced with naive per-flow-step TD or a redesigned padding rule.

The actor samples BC interpolation latents, predicts velocity once, advances by `min(1/F,1-t)`, and evaluates the frozen-weight value network at the advanced latent/time. Value gradients reach the velocity through the latent input; value parameter gradients do not enter the actor objective. This **interpolation-local actor loss** differs from the paper's literal reversed-trajectory description. BC loss masks padded actions and is combined with actor and expectile critic losses in a single Adam update.

Target parameters are blended from the **pre-update** online and target parameters. Thus the first target update after identical initialization leaves targets unchanged. Evaluation uses target_actor EMA with coefficient .999. `temperature=0` selects the EMA actor but does not eliminate initial Gaussian noise. Forward Euler takes F steps, and clips only final decoded actions to `[-1,1]`.

Finite Euler reversal is not an exact inverse: cycle residuals are measured with un-clipped reverse and forward trajectories. No claim of zero discretization error or zero total target variance is made.

## Profiles and manifest

`configs/rql_ogbench_manifest.json` contains all **50 separately trained tasks**, including task-specific suffixes verified from the pinned registration loops, each with four fixed local seeds 0–3. Paper literal seed identities are unavailable. Paper Table 1 scores and 95% intervals are transcribed for every task, including low and zero scores. The aggregate reference score is 56 [54,57] percent; this is a paper value, not a measured local result.

The source launch file contains ten default-task commands. Defaults are task1 for locomotion, task2 for cube/scene, and task4 for puzzle-3x3/4x4; they are aliases, not substitutes for five separately trained goals. Task-specific alpha, expectile, rho, discount, chunk horizon, full four-layer 512-wide networks, ten heads, and ten flow steps are carried into each entry. OGBench 1.2.0 computes single-task reward and success before the action; dataset relabeling uses the current state.

Two fidelity budgets remain explicit:

- `official_code`: 1,000,000 updates, directly from `hyperparameters.sh`.
- `paper_budget`: 2,000,000 updates, from Appendix C Table 2. Appendix C's description of BDPO as 1M additional warmup and twice the total compute is also inconsistent with a blanket 2M table; source cannot resolve historical execution from these statements.

The harness chooses the final checkpoint, without best-seed or best-evaluation-checkpoint selection. The paper does not state an exact checkpoint-selection rule or confidence-interval estimator; these fields are null with reasons. Dataset checksums and exact processed counts are null until download and recorded per run. Both 100M datasets require all 100 train shards, their validation partners, and explicit manifests; replacement every 1000 updates follows the source.

Toy configs use smaller explicitly labeled diagnostic networks. They are not OGBench fidelity settings. Native value outputs remain expectile/pessimistic quantities; they must not be directly identified with unregularized rollout `Q^pi`. Single-candidate deterministic remaining generation gives a constant ideal continuation value along an individual path; upward native-value curves are not a correctness criterion.

## Verification

`tests/test_rql.py` compares local and independently imported pinned upstream implementations with identical initialization, batches and JAX keys, for h=1 and h=3. It checks loss components, every gradient leaf, three full Adam/target/RNG updates, both actor sampling choices, actor/critic gradient separation, old-parameter target order, complete serialization and next-update equality. Separate regressions test terminal bandit reward retention, zero bootstrap, absence of latent clipping, traced-action preservation, continuation from every native step, and nonzero finite-step cycle residual.

Numerical tolerance is 2e-6 absolute/relative for float32 parity; exact equality is required for initialization and same-local-implementation checkpoint continuation. Tests use small networks to keep the reference checks inexpensive. This tests kernel semantics, not paper-scale learning performance; full-budget runs retain the original network dimensions.

On 2026-10-28 the ten RQL checks passed on the fresh JAX CPU backend in 61.03 s (`/home/dmsdmswns/doublemdp_outputs/rql_cpu_parity.log`). The small-network cycle RMSE was 0.0189190786. All 50 registry specifications matched task numbers and pre-action reward timing. Representative antmaze-large/task1 and scene/task2 environments reset, stepped, and rendered 200x200 RGB frames using the EGL setting; actual observation/action dimensions were 29/8 and 40/5. Reports and frames are in `/home/dmsdmswns/doublemdp_outputs/rql_environment_checks/`. EGL emitted device-node permission warnings while still returning valid rendered frames; this rendering check alone does not establish GPU compute execution.

The same ten checks also passed on physical GPU 1, an RTX A6000, under `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1 JAX_PLATFORMS=cuda` in 117.93 s (`rql_gpu_parity.log`). Same-backend upstream/local parity met the declared tolerance. The small-config GPU cycle RMSE was 0.0188938938; CPU and GPU rounding are not claimed bit-identical. Full native-size synthetic-shape timing (B256, four 512-wide layers, ten value heads, ten flow steps) measured 3.7991 ms/update for antmaze dimensions 29/8 and 3.0095 ms/update for scene dimensions 40/5, over 100 synchronized steady updates after warmup. Peak JAX allocations were about 481 MB and 471 MB respectively. These are compute measurements, not benchmark performance; dataset sampling, evaluation and simulator time are excluded. Detailed JSON artifacts are `/home/dmsdmswns/doublemdp_outputs/rql_profile_antmaze.json` and `rql_profile_scene.json`.

### Point-maze transition adapter and corrected diagnostic evidence

The source's episode-start sampler excludes the terminal row. This is compatible with OGBench's current-state task rewards inside task-agnostic trajectories, but the proposed maze stores positive success reward on its final **transition**. Initial maze runs using the unextended source sampler therefore excluded every positive terminal reward at h=1. Their apparent task success does not establish environment-credit learning; those artifacts are retained with `SCIENTIFIC_LIMITATIONS.json` and excluded from credit-propagation claims.

The explicitly labeled toy-only `transition_h1` adapter draws a trajectory uniformly and a transition uniformly **including** its final row. It supplies the two observation rows `[observation_i,next_observation_i]`, action rows `[action_i,action_i]`, reward rows `[reward_i,0]`, and repeated original mask/boundary indicators. The unchanged h=1 RQL loss receives the saved terminal reward, zero bootstrap at real success, and bootstrap at the actual successor for logging timeouts. It does not query the simulator or oracle. OGBench sampling remains exactly source-compatible. Corrected runs use names `rql_maze_transition_*`, and the adapter is part of checkpoint resume validation.

Evaluation also excludes invalid physical start states and gives zero continuation to genuine terminal starts. A near-goal probe originally at (.86,.55), inside the goal's .055 radius, was moved to (.84,.55). These are diagnostic corrections, not algorithm changes. New tests check terminal/invalid-start handling and all fixed probe locations. Earlier atlas data from the old probe must not be used as calibrated policy-value evidence.

### Trained cycle and latent-distribution diagnostics

`scripts/rql_cycle_report.py` measures finite-Euler reverse/forward cycles at 256 fixed saved transition endpoints (seed 731), across all ten saved training checkpoints. It uses the online actor in both directions without clipping. At update 10,000 the bandit cycle RMSE was 0.0862704 for full reversal and 0.0429317 for the source continuous/discrete duration mixture. The corrected point-maze run measured 0.0951822 and 0.0523548 respectively. Raw fixed inputs, reconstructed/cycled latents, residuals, JSON reports and PNG/PDF curves live in each run's `cycle_diagnostic/` directory. These nonzero measurements directly limit the idealized exact-reversal argument; they do not on their own establish a downstream performance cause.

`scripts/rql_latent_shift.py` saves actual online/EMA generated latents, reverse-reconstructed critic inputs, and BC interpolation inputs at the nearest native times to 0, .25, .5, .75, and 1. It uses the same saved-state cohort and Gaussian noise tape. Pooled sliced Wasserstein distances describe marginal latent shift; they do not assert conditional distribution equality or trajectory correspondence. Native expectile-head means are plotted separately and explicitly not equated to unregularized Monte Carlo return. Outputs are in each run's `latent_shift/` directory.

### Continuation history at fixed checkpoints

`scripts/rql_continuation_history.py` evaluates corrected point-maze snapshots at updates 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000, using the four fixed probes, one fixed Gaussian flow seed per probe, and identical future-policy seed tapes across native steps and checkpoints. Each probe uses 16 independent simulation continuations with a 100-step zero-tail horizon. Native head means and ensemble spreads appear in separate panels; their difference from unregularized Monte Carlo return is explicitly marked incomparable, not a calibration error. The raw latents, actual decoded continuations, individual returns, unfinished fractions, noise tapes, and exact snapshot hashes are stored with PNG/PDF/HTML outputs in `rql_maze_transition_seed0/continuation_history/`. `--regenerate` redraws them without loading a policy.

The actual deterministic continuation endpoints differed from the recorded final action by at most 1.79e-7 across tested probes/steps. Reusing future simulation noise yielded constant measured continuation means along each individual flow path, as expected. At the fork, truncated mean return changed from 0 at update 1,000 (all rollouts unfinished) to .4335 at 5,000 and .6786 at 10,000; these are one training seed's conditional diagnostic estimates, not training-seed confidence intervals.
